# Mistake practice contract — v2

Practice starts immediately before an owned, completed, non-provisional mistake whose saved best/played searches pass the shared legal-evidence checks. The player retains the original colour. The source initial FEN and **all** preceding moves are frozen, then every source/practice move is replayed server-side. The source game and review are never edited. Optional weakness links must refer to an owned saved snapshot containing this exact run/ply.

A session has successive attempts. Each attempt lasts at most **three player decisions** and their replies, or ends at a legal terminal position. It does not require winning a whole game or choosing a prescribed engine move. Completed attempts keep their history, grading evidence and assistance. Retry creates a new attempt. Undo during play returns to the previous player decision; hints and undo make that attempt assisted. Reload resumes the persisted state. Cancelling closes the current attempt; retry starts fresh.

Objectives are selected from verified evidence, with explicit bounds:

- A positive mate score: retain a verified forced mate at every decision, or deliver checkmate. Mate distances stay separate from centipawns.
- A best centipawn score of at least +300: preserve an advantage, retaining at least +100 at each chosen move and losing no more than 100 centipawns relative to the best move.
- Otherwise: improve decision safety, avoiding a loss greater than 100 centipawns relative to the best move, or a newly allowed/lost forced mate, at each decision. This includes positions already worse; it does not claim a drawable or winning result.

Full-strength grading starts with independent 250,000- and 750,000-node searches (5/8-second search ceilings) at skill 20 for both unrestricted best and the exact chosen move. Both budgets must agree on acceptance and objective retention; absolute winning centipawn estimates can grow with search depth, so their difference alone does not reject a decision when relative loss and the objective are stable; the decision’s relative centipawn loss (best minus chosen, clamped at zero) must stay within 75 between budgets, mate scores must agree, root moves/PVs must be legal, scores exact and engine/settings compatible. On execution two/three, both node ceilings double/quadruple (up to one/three million); their search time ceilings remain 5/8 seconds. Distinct budgets prevent a retry from endlessly reusing one unstable cached pair. Unstable/incomplete evidence retries at most three times and ends **ungraded**, never as an incorrect chess answer. Any legal alternative meeting the objective is accepted. A restricted centipawn search may discover a stronger candidate than the unrestricted estimate; its loss is zero. A claimed unavoidable losing mate contradicted by a surviving candidate remains ungraded pending verification. After the third practical reply, fresh two-budget evidence also checks the final advantage/mate objective. Starting objectives and checks are frozen per attempt, so retry under changed engine evidence cannot reinterpret an old result. An accepted decision completes early at a terminal position; a draw is acceptable only if its checked evaluation still meets the objective. Practice automatically claims a draw at the current threefold repetition or fifty-move boundary, and recognizes insufficient-material draws. Recorded source games may legitimately continue past an earlier unclaimed draw. Repetition keys preserve side/castling rights and include en-passant rights only when an en-passant capture is legal; counters are not part of that key. Checkmate takes precedence. There is no mate-to-centipawn conversion.

The practical opponent uses skill 6, 30,000 nodes and a one-second ceiling. Its evaluation is never grading evidence. It is a bounded engine setting, not an estimated human rating. Undo preserves a journal and the earlier grading evidence while removing that decision from the current continuation. Post-attempt review explains each player's decision, shows the checked best/played searches and legal candidate continuation, identifies assisted/unassisted outcomes and links to the exact original review and supplied weakness snapshot.

Engine work is persisted as initialization, turn and hint jobs. Each has a two-minute lease, 90-second processing deadline, at most three executions and a session-version/attempt barrier. A half-second durable-state watchdog cancels engine work when its job/session is superseded, cancelled or deleted. Stale, cancelled or deleted work cannot publish. A browser command includes the current attempt and session version; concurrent commands serialize under the existing owner advisory lock. Hint answers/evaluations do not appear during unassisted play. Hints intentionally reveal one legal candidate and count as assistance. Pending work can be undone or cancelled.

Admission permits at most two active sessions, forty new sessions and two hundred practice engine jobs per owner per rolling day, and at most 1,000 pending practice jobs globally. Usage records survive imported-data deletion so deletion cannot reset this quota. The interactive worker alternates position-review and practice jobs, sharing the existing bounded Stockfish pool with the fair background worker. Supported process limits and measured scheduling still require the final release audit.

This is the design/implementation contract. Fresh fixture, actual-engine and browser evidence belongs in `docs/verification.md`; this file alone does not prove a passing gate.

Rules-library reference: [Gera.Chess maintainer documentation](https://github.com/Geras1mleo/Chess), also bundled in the locked 1.2.0 package. Automatic draw rules are optional; practice retains its legal/checkmate/stalemate adjudication and uses explicit current-position draw claims. Standard dead-material cases are king only, a lone bishop/knight, or bishops exclusively on one square colour. Two knights are not declared dead: a legal cooperative mate is possible. Gera 1.2.0 incorrectly labels them insufficient; the guarded cases have regression coverage. This conservative rule does not claim to prove every exotic blocked dead position. Source replay retains the existing legal move path.
